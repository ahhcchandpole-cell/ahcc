NEURO REHABILITATION AT AHHC

Recovery ka Goal Sirf Movement Nahi, Better Function Hai

Neurological condition ke baad movement, balance, strength, coordination ya walking mein difficulty ho sakti hai.

Neuro rehabilitation mein patient ki current functional ability ko assess karke exercises aur functional activities ko gradually progress kiya jata hai.

Har patient ka recovery pattern different hota hai, isliye rehabilitation plan bhi individual assessment ke according decide kiya jata hai.

WHO MAY BENEFIT

Kin Patients ke liye Neuro Rehabilitation Useful Ho Sakti Hai?

Neurological conditions ke baad functional recovery aur independence improve karne ke liye structured rehabilitation useful ho sakti hai.

🧠

Stroke Rehabilitation

Stroke ke baad weakness, balance, walking, coordination aur daily activities par focused rehabilitation.

⚖️

Balance Problems

Balance control aur safe movement improve karne ke liye progressive balance and functional training.

🚶

Walking & Gait Problems

Walking pattern, mobility, weight shifting aur functional gait training par focus.

💪

Weakness & Reduced Mobility

Muscle strength, movement control aur daily functional activities ko gradually improve karne par focus.

ASSESSMENT FIRST

Rehabilitation Start Karne Se Pehle Assessment Zaroori Hai

Neuro rehabilitation ka plan patient ki condition aur current functional ability ko samajhne ke baad decide kiya jata hai.

01. Movement Assessment Movement quality aur available range ko understand karna.
02. Strength & Control Muscle strength aur movement control ko assess karna.
03. Balance & Mobility Sitting, standing, balance aur mobility ko evaluate karna.
04. Functional Ability Daily activities aur functional limitations ko understand karna.
OUR REHABILITATION APPROACH

Neuro Rehab mein Hum Kis Cheez par Focus Karte Hain?

Rehabilitation ko patient ke current level se start karke gradually progress kiya jata hai. Har exercise ka purpose functional improvement se connected hona chahiye.

01
Movement Training Controlled movement aur motor function ko improve karne ke liye targeted exercises.
02
Balance Training Sitting, standing aur dynamic balance ko progressively train karna.
03
Gait & Walking Training Safe walking, weight shifting aur gait-related functional activities par kaam karna.
04
Functional Training Bed mobility, transfers, standing aur daily activities ko practice karna.
FUNCTIONAL RECOVERY

Exercise ko Daily Life se Connect Karna Zaroori Hai

Neuro rehabilitation ka ultimate objective sirf clinic mein exercise complete karna nahi hai.

Patient ko gradually un activities ke liye prepare karna important hai jo unki real daily life mein required hoti hain.

Bed Mobility Position change aur bed se movement ko practice karna.
Sit to Stand Sitting se standing tak controlled transition.
Walking Appropriate support ke saath progressive walking practice.
Daily Activities Functional independence ko support karne wali activities.
BEYOND THE CLINIC

Recovery Sirf Session Tak Limited Nahi Honi Chahiye

Neuro rehabilitation mein regular practice aur consistency important ho sakti hai.

Suitable patients aur families ko home-based exercises, positioning, safe mobility aur daily activity practice ke regarding guidance di ja sakti hai.

Goal: Patient ko jitna possible ho, safe aur meaningful functional independence ki taraf progress karna.
EVERY PATIENT IS DIFFERENT

Neuro Recovery Ek Step-by-Step Process Hai

Neurological recovery ka timeline aur outcome har patient mein different ho sakta hai.

Isliye rehabilitation ka focus realistic goals, measurable progress aur patient ki individual needs ke according hona chahiye.
