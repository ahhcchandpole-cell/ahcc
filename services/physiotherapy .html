PHYSIOTHERAPY AT AHHC

Physiotherapy Designed Around Your Functional Goals

Physiotherapy at Aleem Health and Hijama Centre is focused on helping patients manage pain, restore movement, improve strength and return to meaningful daily activities.

Treatment is planned according to the patient's condition, physical assessment, functional limitations and rehabilitation goals rather than following a single approach for every patient.

OUR PHYSIOTHERAPY SERVICES

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Specialities

Our physiotherapy services cover musculoskeletal, neurological and cardiopulmonary rehabilitation needs.

🦴

Orthopaedic Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy for musculoskeletal pain, joint stiffness, sports and other injuries, post-operative recovery and movement limitations.

  • Joint and muscle pain
  • Post-operative rehabilitation
  • Mobility and strengthening
  • Functional recovery
🧠

Neuro Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for neurological conditions with emphasis on movement, balance, coordination and functional independence.

  • Stroke rehabilitation
  • Balance and gait training
  • Strength and mobility
  • Functional training
❤️

Cardiac Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation and exercise-based care designed to improve functional capacity and support cardiovascular recovery.

  • Exercise capacity assessment
  • Supervised exercise
  • Functional conditioning
  • Cardiovascular rehabilitation
🫁

Pulmonary Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy and rehabilitation approaches supporting breathing, exercise tolerance and functional activity.

  • Breathing exercises
  • Exercise training
  • Airway management where indicated
  • Functional conditioning
WHO MAY BENEFIT

Physiotherapy for Different Stages of Recovery

Physiotherapy may be useful for people experiencing pain, reduced mobility, weakness or limitations in everyday physical activities.

Pain & stiffness Support for movement-related pain and stiffness.
Post-operative recovery Progressive rehabilitation after appropriate surgical procedures.
Reduced mobility Exercise and functional training to improve mobility and independence.
Reduced exercise capacity Structured rehabilitation for suitable cardiopulmonary patients.
