REHABILITATION KO SIMPLE SAMJHEIN

Cardiac Rehabilitation kya hota hai?

Heart-related condition ya treatment ke baad sirf rest karna hi recovery ka solution nahi hota. Appropriate patients mein structured rehabilitation body ko gradually activity aur exercise ke liye adapt karne mein help kar sakti hai.

Program patient ki condition, symptoms, functional capacity aur medical clearance ke according plan kiya jata hai.

Goal simple hai — safe movement, better exercise tolerance aur daily life mein better functional confidence.

WHO MAY BENEFIT

Kin Patients ke liye Rehabilitation Useful Ho Sakti Hai?

Har patient ka rehabilitation plan individual assessment aur medical suitability ke according decide kiya jata hai.

WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Rehabilitation Program mein kya hota hai?

Rehabilitation ka objective sirf exercise karwana nahi hai. Exercise ko patient ki current capacity ke according dose aur progress kiya jata hai.

01. Assessment Initial functional status aur exercise capacity ko understand karna.
02. Exercise Prescription Patient ke level ke according appropriate exercise intensity decide karna.
03. Supervised Exercise Walking, cycling, conditioning aur functional exercises ka structured use.
04. Gradual Progression Exercise tolerance improve hone par activity ko progressively advance karna.
ASSESSMENT & MONITORING

Rehabilitation ko Measure bhi karna zaroori hai

Progress ko objective functional measures aur appropriate monitoring ke through track kiya ja sakta hai.

6-Minute Walk Test

Suitable patients mein functional exercise capacity assess karne ke liye.

Heart Rate & Blood Pressure

Exercise response ko understand karne aur appropriate monitoring ke liye.

SpO₂ Monitoring

Selected cardiopulmonary patients mein oxygen saturation monitoring as clinically appropriate.

Borg Rating of Perceived Exertion

Patient ko exercise intensity aur exertion ko understand karne mein help karne ke liye.

RECOVERY IS A PROCESS

Recovery ko Step-by-Step Build Kiya Jata Hai

Rehabilitation mein har patient ki journey different hoti hai. Isliye exercise intensity aur progression ko individual response ke according adjust kiya jata hai.

Agar aapko cardiac ya respiratory condition ke baad activity, walking, stamina ya exercise tolerance ko lekar concern hai, toh professional assessment se starting point decide kiya ja sakta hai.
