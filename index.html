ABOUT AHHC

Healthcare That Brings Care, Expertise & Rehabilitation Together

Aleem Health and Hijama Centre is a multidisciplinary healthcare centre committed to providing accessible, personalised and compassionate care under one roof.

Our centre brings together medical consultation, physiotherapy, rehabilitation and Hijama therapy with a team of healthcare professionals from different areas of expertise.

At AHHC, we believe that healthcare should look beyond treating a single complaint. Our approach focuses on understanding the individual, identifying their healthcare needs and guiding them towards appropriate professional care.

With a patient-centred environment, experienced professionals and a focus on responsible healthcare practices, AHHC aims to make quality healthcare more approachable for individuals and families.

PHYSIOTHERAPY & REHABILITATION

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation in Chandpole, Jaipur

AHHC provides comprehensive physiotherapy and rehabilitation services for orthopaedic, neurological, cardiac and pulmonary conditions.

Our approach focuses on pain management, movement, strength, mobility, exercise capacity and functional independence. Each rehabilitation programme is planned according to the individual's condition, functional needs and recovery goals.
