ABOUT AHHC

Healthcare With a Patient-Centred Approach

Aleem Health and Hijama Centre is a healthcare centre located in Chandpole, Jaipur, providing healthcare, Hijama, physiotherapy and rehabilitation services.

Our aim is to provide accessible and professional healthcare while keeping the needs and wellbeing of every patient at the centre of care.
